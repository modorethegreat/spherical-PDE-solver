About the model

The globe shows final snapshots of an idealized shallow-water layer. Shorter radiative times restore the imposed dayside–nightside thickness contrast faster. Shorter drag times damp the winds faster. Each field uses a fixed color scale across the sweep.

The thickness offset tracks the phase of the first longitudinal harmonic at the equator. Thickness is a model quantity in metres. Reproduce the experiment · Download fields and diagnostics
